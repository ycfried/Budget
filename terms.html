STAGE — Terms of Use

What STAGE is

STAGE is a tracking tool for guided spending, saving, and giving. It tracks a weekly spending amount, purchases, an Extra Fund for leftovers, and savings. It is a tracking tool, not a bank, not a financial advisor, and not a tax service. Nothing in the app is financial advice.

Your account

  • You sign in with an email address and password. Keep your password private; you are responsible for activity under your account.
  • A shared record has one admin and any number of members. The admin changes policy, exclusions, and fund allocations; members can ask for fund money and the admin approves or declines.
  • You join a shared record with its invite code. Do not share an invite code with people you do not want in your record — anyone with the code can join as a member.

Bank and email connections (optional)

  • Bank connections are read-only through Plaid. The app cannot move money, make payments, or change anything at your bank. Bank data can be delayed or incomplete: a purchase shows as pending until the bank confirms it, and the bank's record is authoritative when the two disagree.
  • Email alerts are read-only through Gmail. The app reads only transaction alert emails from your connected bank (for example, American Express Large Purchase alerts). Email purchases stay provisional until the bank confirms them.
  • You can disconnect a bank or an email account at any time from inside the app. Disconnecting a bank stops future syncing; past purchases stay in your record. A disconnected bank stays listed as "previously connected" for the record, with no token kept.

What you must not do

  • Do not use the app to break the law, to abuse other users, or to interfere with the service.
  • Do not try to access another record's data, forge records, or bypass the app's access controls.

Availability

The app is provided "as is." We do not promise it will always be available, error-free, or free forever. Connected services (your bank, Plaid, Google) can change, delay, or interrupt data, and that is outside our control. If the service changes or closes, we will say so in the app where practical.

Your data and deletion

Your data belongs to your shared record. You can delete your account from inside the app: your memberships are removed, any record left with no members is deleted with its data, and your sign-in is removed. Records with remaining members keep their data.
