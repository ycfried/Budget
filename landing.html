How it works

1

One number to watch. STAGE shows you one number: what you can spend this week. You set the amount together. It goes down as you spend, and starts fresh every week.

2

Giving, automatic. When money comes in, ten percent is set aside for giving, automatically. STAGE works out how much you owe and how much you’ve already given, so you always know where you stand. You can change the amount, or turn it off entirely.

3

Saving, your cushion. Put a little aside when you have extra, and dip into it when something comes up — a big bill, a slow month, whatever it is. It’s always there, quiet in the background.

4

Every account in one place. Connect your bank and purchases appear on their own, as they happen. If you have more than one account, they all land here together — no more opening one bank app after another to find a transaction. The app can see purchases — it never moves money.

5

No adding things up, no categories. No categories to create and manage — nobody has the time or the head space for that. Paid cash, or something hasn’t shown up yet? One tap on the plus, an amount, and a few words, and you’re done.

6

A shared history. Every change is recorded in an audit log both people can read, so the numbers are never a mystery.
