Household Budget

A private budget for two people sharing one household — one weekly allowance, one shared record, no surprises.

Sign in

How it works

One weekly allowance. You set a single amount to spend each Sunday-to-Saturday week. What you spend is counted against that week; spending over it carries into the next week.

An Extra Fund for leftovers. Money you do not spend in a week becomes the Extra Fund after a short holding period. Either person can ask to use it; the household admin approves each use.

Bank purchases, read-only. Connect a bank account and purchases appear automatically. The app can see purchases — it never moves money.

A shared history. Every change is recorded in an audit log both people can read, so the numbers are never a mystery.
