Transactions (shared)

Everything spent on the household cards, from all sources. Tap any purchase for its full detail — what week it counts in, where it came from, and its actions. Use the filters and search to find a purchase, and exclude anything that should not count toward the week.

    Add a manual purchase (provisional)

    Record a purchase by hand. It counts as provisional until the bank confirms it.

    Never enter a full card number.

    Bank connection (read-only)

    Connect a bank account and pull its transactions into this budget. Read-only — it never moves money. Your bank login token is stored encrypted, never in plain text, and never leaves the server. If this button reports "not configured," the bank connection is not set up on the server yet.

    Automatic sync: once enabled, the bank notifies the app when new purchases appear, and the app pulls them in on its next refresh — no need to tap Sync each time.

    Settings (admin only)

    Change the weekly allowance and add one-off increases for this week. Only the admin can change these, and every change is written to the activity log.

    Savings (manual attestation only)

    The app never moves money. This records what you observed in the real savings account. Attestations are not spending, not income, and never change the Extra Fund.

    Extra Fund requests

    Ask for money from the Extra Fund. The admin approves or declines each request, and the decision is written to the activity log.
