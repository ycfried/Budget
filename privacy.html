STAGE — Privacy Policy

STAGE is a tracking tool for guided spending, saving, and giving. Two people — you and the person you invite — share one record. This policy explains what we collect, what we do with it, and how you can remove it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and password (for sign-in only). Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider, never in plain text in the app.
  • Your data: the weekly spending amount you set, purchases (merchant, amount, date, card suffix), the Extra Fund ledger and requests, savings records, and an audit trail of changes. This is the data you and the other member enter or connect.
  • Bank connections (optional): if you connect a bank, we use Plaid, a bank-linking service. You type your bank username and password into Plaid's own window — STAGE never sees them. What we store is an encrypted, read-only token that can only pull transactions. It cannot move money, and it cannot change anything at your bank.
  • Email alerts (optional): if you connect Gmail, we use Google's sign-in and ask for read-only access. The app reads only transaction alert emails from your connected bank (for example, American Express Large Purchase alerts), so a purchase can show up before the bank reports it. It never reads your whole mailbox, never sends email, and never changes email.
  • Nothing else: no location, no contacts, no photos, no advertising identifiers.

How your data is kept apart

Every shared record is isolated: members only ever see their own record. Nobody can see another record, and joining requires an invite code.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data, to anyone, ever.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your purchases or spending data to train models or to market anything to you.

The services that touch your data

  • Supabase hosts the app's database, sign-in, and servers.
  • Plaid handles bank connections (read-only transactions).
  • Google handles Gmail sign-in and read-only alert reads, only if you connect email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web app itself.

Each of these has its own privacy policy. We do not control their policies, and we send them only what they need to do their job.

How long we keep data

Your data stays until you delete it. Purchases you remove are excluded, not erased, so the week's history stays honest; the underlying entry remains in your record until the record is deleted.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app. Deleting your account removes your memberships; any record left with no members is deleted along with its data, and your sign-in is removed. If other members remain, that record and its data stay for them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects your data, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the new version.
