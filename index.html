    Open question — FIN-REFUND-001

    The refund-credit / surplus policy is unresolved. Financial outputs that depend on an excess refund in a week with no debt and no charges are not production-certified until the owner chooses the rule. See OPEN_QUESTIONS.md. This demo does not silently resolve it.
