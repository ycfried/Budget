How it works

Guided spending. Set a weekly spending amount together. What you spend is tracked against the week, so you always know where you stand.

Saving, built in. Money you do not spend in a week becomes savings after a short holding period. Either person can ask to use it; the admin approves each use.

Giving, automatic. A share of what comes in is set aside for giving. You choose the share; STAG does the counting.

Bank purchases, read-only. Connect a bank account and purchases appear automatically. The app can see purchases — it never moves money.

A shared history. Every change is recorded in an audit log both people can read, so the numbers are never a mystery.
